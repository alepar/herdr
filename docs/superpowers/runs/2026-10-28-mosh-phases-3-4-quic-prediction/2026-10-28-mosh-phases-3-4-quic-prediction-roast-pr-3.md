But the 4-prediction design (2026-10-28-mosh-phases-3-4-quic-prediction--4-prediction-design.md:75) states this exact residual and accepts it: "The first epoch of a connection is visible (nothing breaks before it), so a client that attaches or reconnects while a pane already waits at a no-echo prompt still paints that text until it is judged wrong", with line 76 noting the R8 test still covers clearing a first-epoch key. The finding's premise that the design is silent fails. Prediction is opt-in and off by default, and the paint is on the user's own screen for about one RTT plus 50 ms plus one render.
